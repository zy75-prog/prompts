Study radiation tolerance of a material I specify using MLIPs and selected DFT
benchmarks.

Workflow:
1. Obtain and verify its Materials Project structure.
2. Relax the pristine primitive cell's lattice and atomic positions at zero
   pressure.
3. Use supercell dimensions I specify, or propose a small initial supercell
   based on the relaxed structure and available resources. Report its
   dimensions and atom count for approval, then relax atomic positions with
   the cell fixed.
4. Search for isolated interstitial ground-state and low-energy
   configurations of each species, without a vacancy.
5. Calculate isolated vacancy and interstitial migration paths for each
   species using fixed-cell climbing-image NEB.
6. Calculate close- and intermediate-distance Frenkel-pair recombination
   paths using fixed-cell relaxation and climbing-image NEB.
7. Benchmark selected MLIP paths with DFT single-point calculations on their
   image geometries, including endpoints, when explicitly authorized.
8. Evaluate displacement thresholds using fixed-cell recoil MD only after
   the short-range collision model is adequately validated.

After relaxing the pristine bulk lattice, keep supercell lattice vectors fixed
while allowing atomic positions to relax or evolve. Use the same bulk cell for
defect endpoints and all NEB images. For finite-temperature recoil MD,
equilibrate the pristine bulk at the target temperature and pressure first,
then fix the cell during recoil. Keep the approved supercell size unless I
authorize a change; report finite-size limitations.

For displacement thresholds, adapt the recoil protocol in the reference below
using MLIP forces. Start from relaxed bulk at 0 K with zero velocities, give
one selected atom recoil kinetic energy in a specified direction, and run
fixed-cell NVE MD. Test inequivalent sites and multiple directions. Run 1 ps,
then relax atomic positions; the paper uses a 0.03 eV/angstrom force
tolerance. If the crystal heals, raise recoil energy in 5 eV steps, then
refine the first defect-producing interval in 1 eV steps. Check nearby
energies for nonmonotonic defect survival rather than assuming a single sharp
threshold.

Confirm each candidate threshold with a separate 2 ps run from the same
pristine recoil initial conditions, followed by relaxation. Require defects to
survive both durations. Check timestep convergence and total-energy drift; the
paper's 1 fs timestep is a reference, not an automatic MLIP setting. Report
direction-specific threshold brackets and the minimum found among sampled
directions; distinguish these from directional averages. A 1 eV scan
resolution does not establish total accuracy, especially for an unvalidated
collision potential. Retain recoil-generated defects for recombination NEB.

Before classifying defects, align the relaxed structure to pristine bulk to
remove rigid translation under periodic boundaries. Use species-aware atom
matching and site occupancies to identify vacancies, interstitials, antisites,
and replacement chains. Distinguish identical-atom exchanges from surviving
defects. Check healing with aligned structure, occupancies, and energy, not
the primary recoil atom's displacement alone.

For isolated interstitials, add one atom to the approved pristine supercell.
Sample crystallographically appropriate voids, including tetrahedral or
octahedral candidates where applicable, other open sites, split-interstitial
orientations, and random perturbations. Verify coordination after relaxation;
do not assign a site type from its starting label alone. Relax atomic
positions, group equivalent final structures, rank distinct minima within each
species at identical composition, and expand sampling until the low-energy
ranking stabilizes. Retain distinct low-energy metastable states as well as
the ground-state candidate, reporting their relative energies and structures.
Use prior findings as checks, not restrictions. Label the ground state as the
lowest-energy state found under the chosen model and cell, not a proven global
minimum.

For isolated migration, relax vacancies on inequivalent sites and test
lattice-atom hops into the vacancy. Sample interstitial hops between distinct
low-energy configurations and equivalent sites. Check for antisite creation.
Explore alternative mechanisms, including interstitialcy and concerted motion.
Separate isolated-defect migration barriers from pair recombination barriers.

Seed Frenkel pairs from low-energy interstitial states and recoil-generated
structures. Focus on close and intermediate vacancy-interstitial separations
within the approved cell; do not require very distant pairs or a larger cell.
Use isolated-defect migration to characterize the distant-defect limit, while
not assuming finite-distance interactions vanish. Report initial and relaxed
shortest periodic separations, pair energies, healing, and antisite formation.
Compare distances and orientations where possible. For surviving pairs, use
successive hops through independently relaxed intermediate states when they
exist. Otherwise label the path as direct recombination. Let all atoms relax;
track vacancy motion rather than assuming or artificially forcing it to stay.

Use MACE-OMAT-0 medium by default, with an explicit checkpoint path, version,
and checksum. Keep previous MACE-MPA-0 results separately labeled. Search
several plausible paths and retain distinct competitive mechanisms; the lowest
converged sampled barrier is not proof of the globally easiest path.

For DFT benchmarking, use only the final MLIP path selected for each studied
migration or recombination process. Compare alternative paths with MLIP only;
select the final path using its converged barrier, physical mechanism, and
path quality, and state the selection basis. Do not send alternative paths to
DFT. After approval of DFT settings and scope, calculate static energies and
forces on all images of that final path, including both endpoints. Use
consistent settings and relative energies. Compare the DFT and MLIP profiles,
barriers, and DFT forces. Call this a DFT energy profile along the selected
MLIP path, not a DFT minimum-energy path. If disagreement or large DFT forces
warrants endpoint relaxation or DFT NEB refinement, propose that separately.
Do not infer charge states from ordinary MACE calculations or call its
predictions neutral-defect barriers. Specify charge states for DFT; charged-
supercell corrections and carrier effects require explicit treatment. Report
limitations from omitted charge-state changes and from selecting the path with
MLIP alone.
